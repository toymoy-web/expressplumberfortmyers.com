Snaked it and it clogged again a few weeks later? A cable or snake punches a hole through a clog and leaves the buildup lining the pipe walls behind. If a drain keeps backing up on a schedule, or a plumber mentioned root intrusion after an earlier snaking call, hydro jetting is usually the fix that actually lasts. It clears the whole diameter of the pipe instead of just reopening a channel through it.

Why Fort Myers Lines Need It

The Local Cause

Fort Myers has a genuinely real hydro jetting market, not just a generic upsell. Established oaks and other large trees across the city send roots into sewer lines searching for moisture, especially during the dry season, and once roots get a foothold they regrow through the same crack within months of a basic snaking. On top of that, the local water supply draws from the Southwest Florida limestone aquifer. The mineral content in that water gradually narrows pipe openings with scale buildup over years, the same way it shortens water heater lifespans (see water heater installation & replacement). Older Fort Myers neighborhoods running original cast iron alongside newer PVC construction see both problems overlap most often.

How It Works

What Hydro Jetting Actually Does

Camera Inspection First

We run a sewer camera through the line before jetting to confirm the blockage type and check for any cracks jetting could worsen.

High-Pressure Water Jet

A specialized nozzle sends water through the line at high pressure, scouring grease, roots, mineral scale, and sludge off the full pipe wall.

Root Cutting Attachment

For heavy root intrusion, a rotating cutter head clears root mass before the jetting pass finishes the job.

Post-Jet Camera Check

A second camera pass confirms the line is clear and flags any structural damage a repeat clog might be hiding.

Snaking vs. Jetting

Why A Repeat Snaking Call Doesn't Fix It

Snaking Alone

  • Punches a hole through the clog
  • Leaves grease and scale on pipe walls
  • Root mass regrows through the same gap
  • Often needs repeating within months

Hydro Jetting

  • Clears the full pipe diameter
  • Removes grease, scale, and root mass
  • Camera-verified before and after
  • Longer-lasting result for recurring clogs

Before You Call

What To Check First

  • Note which drains are affected. A single slow drain is often local to that fixture; multiple drains backing up at once points to a main line issue.
  • Avoid pouring chemical drain cleaners down a line that's already backing up repeatedly. They rarely clear root intrusion or scale and can make a camera inspection harder to read.
  • If you've had the same drain snaked more than once in the past year, mention that when you call. It changes what we check first.
FAQs

Common Questions

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

We camera-inspect first specifically to check pipe condition. On older or already-cracked pipe, we may recommend a gentler approach or a repair instead of full-pressure jetting.

How is this different from what a snake does?

A snake breaks a path through the clog. Jetting scours the entire interior surface of the pipe, which is why it holds up better against tree roots and grease that regrow or rebuild on the walls.

Do you offer free estimates for hydro jetting?

Yes, for non-emergency calls. We confirm the price after the camera inspection and before any jetting work begins.

Can hydro jetting help with a septic system backup?

It depends on where the blockage sits. If it is in the line between the house and the septic tank, jetting can help; a full septic tank pumping is a separate service for the tank itself.
