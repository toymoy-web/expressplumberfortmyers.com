Common Reasons For A Call

What We Handle

Gas line calls in Fort Myers range from installing a new line for a range, dryer, or pool heater, to repairing an aging line showing corrosion, to relocating a line during a kitchen or outdoor kitchen remodel. Homes converting from electric to gas appliances, or adding a whole-home generator, are a common source of new-line installation calls, especially as generator demand has grown given the area's real hurricane season power outage risk. Any suspected gas leak is treated as a safety-first call, not a routine repair, and is prioritized accordingly. Older homes with original steel gas piping are also more likely to need a line evaluated for corrosion before adding a new appliance.

Signs You Need Service

When To Call

Smell Of Gas

A sulfur or rotten-egg smell near an appliance or line is a safety issue and should be reported immediately.

Hissing Sound Near A Line

An audible hiss near a gas connection or appliance line warrants an immediate inspection.

Appliance Not Igniting Properly

Inconsistent flame or ignition trouble can point to a line or pressure issue rather than the appliance itself.

New Appliance Or Generator Install

Adding a gas range, pool heater, or standby generator often requires a new dedicated line run.

Before You Call

What To Do If You Smell Gas

  • Leave the area immediately if you smell gas strongly. Do not operate light switches, appliances, or anything that could create a spark.
  • Once safely away from the property, call your gas utility's emergency line as well as us, since utility involvement is standard practice for a suspected leak and helps confirm the source quickly.
  • For a new appliance or generator install, have the appliance's BTU rating available when you call, since it affects the line sizing needed.
  • If a pilot light keeps going out on an existing appliance, mention that too, since it can sometimes point to a gas supply issue rather than the appliance itself.
FAQs

Common Questions

Is a gas smell always an emergency?

Treat any noticeable gas smell as a safety issue. Leave the area and call both your gas utility and us right away rather than trying to diagnose it yourself.

How often should older gas lines be inspected?

There's no fixed universal schedule, but homes with original steel piping over 20 to 30 years old benefit from a periodic inspection, especially before adding a new appliance or generator.

Can you run a line for a new generator?

Yes, we install dedicated gas lines for standby generators, sized to the unit's requirements and Fort Myers' real hurricane-season demand for reliable backup power during extended outages.

Do you handle permits for gas line work?

Yes, permitting is handled as part of the job where required, so you don't have to coordinate that separately with the county or city.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, for non-emergency installation and repair work. We confirm the price after inspection.
