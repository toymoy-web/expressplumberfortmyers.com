About Whiskey Creek

A Small Community South Of The City

Whiskey Creek is an unincorporated community in central Lee County. The Census Bureau counts it as a census-designated place. It had 4,842 residents at the 2020 census and 2,678 housing units, covering about 1.62 square miles.

The City of Fort Myers borders it to the north and Cypress Lake lies to the south. The Caloosahatchee River and Whiskey Creek form its western edge, with McGregor across the creek. Villas and Pine Manor are to the east.

Because Whiskey Creek is unincorporated, it is not part of the City of Fort Myers. The 2020 census found 13.8 percent of its housing units vacant, and an empty house can hide a slow leak for a long time.

Practical Notes

Jurisdiction, Moisture, And Empty Homes

City and county lines are not always obvious on a street map. Tanglewood, a former census-designated place next door, was partly annexed by Fort Myers, and the rest was reassigned to Whiskey Creek and McGregor. Before pulling a permit, confirm whether your address falls under Lee County or the City of Fort Myers. Plumbing work is governed by the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), and many jobs can require a permit from the building department that has jurisdiction over your address.

General plumbing note: humid, salt-influenced air can speed up corrosion on exposed supply connectors, shutoff valves, and fittings in garages and utility rooms. Checking them twice a year costs little. A homeowner who is away for weeks should also shut off the main valve before leaving.

FAQs

Whiskey Creek Plumbing Questions

Is Whiskey Creek part of the City of Fort Myers?

No. Whiskey Creek is an unincorporated community in Lee County and a census-designated place. The City of Fort Myers borders it to the north.

Do you serve Whiskey Creek?

Yes. Call with your street address and we will confirm coverage before scheduling anything.

Which government handles plumbing permits at my address?

That depends on whether your address is in unincorporated Lee County or inside the City of Fort Myers. Boundaries near Whiskey Creek are not always obvious, so confirm with the permitting office before work starts.

Is my home on sewer or septic?

Utility setups vary by property. We confirm whether a home is on sewer or septic, and where its water comes from, before recommending work.

What should I do with a water heater that is leaking?

Turn off its power or gas supply, close the cold-water inlet valve, and call us. A leak from the tank itself usually means the heater needs replacing.
