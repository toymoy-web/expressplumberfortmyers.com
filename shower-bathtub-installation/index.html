Common Reasons For This Call

What We Handle

Shower and bathtub calls in Fort Myers range from a straightforward like-for-like replacement to a full walk-in shower conversion for accessibility. Homes with an older tub-shower combo sometimes want to convert to a curbless walk-in shower, which involves real plumbing work beyond just swapping fixtures, including drain relocation and proper slope for water drainage. Hard local water can also accelerate wear on shower valves and fixtures, which is worth factoring into a replacement decision if the existing unit is already showing scale-related issues. Retirees and long-term residents planning to stay in their homes are a common source of accessibility-focused conversion calls, since a walk-in shower removes a real fall hazard that a traditional tub presents over time.

Common Situations

When To Consider This

Cracked Or Leaking Tub

A cracked tub or persistent leak at the drain or overflow often means replacement rather than repair, especially on an older fiberglass unit.

Accessibility Needs

Converting a tub to a curbless walk-in shower is a common request for homeowners planning to age in place.

Outdated Fixtures

Worn valves, low water pressure, or dated finishes are common reasons for a full shower or tub update, especially in homes over 15 to 20 years old.

Remodel Or Renovation

A full bathroom remodel often includes new plumbing rough-in for a repositioned or upgraded shower or tub.

Before You Call

What To Check First

  • Measure your existing tub or shower's dimensions if you know them, since that helps scope whether a like-for-like replacement or a larger change is involved.
  • If considering a walk-in conversion, think about whether drain relocation and floor slope changes are something you've discussed with a contractor already.
  • Note any existing leaks or soft spots around the current unit, since that can indicate hidden water damage worth addressing during the install.
  • If accessibility is a priority, think about grab bar placement and bench seating options, since those are easier to plan for before installation than to add afterward.
FAQs

Common Questions

Can you convert my tub to a walk-in shower?

Yes, this includes the plumbing work for drain relocation and proper slope, not just the fixture swap itself.

Do you handle the tile and surround work too?

We handle the plumbing rough-in and fixture installation; tile and surround finishing is often coordinated with a contractor, which we can help plan around as part of a larger remodel timeline.

How long does a typical installation take?

A like-for-like replacement is often completed in a day; a full walk-in conversion with plumbing changes takes longer depending on scope.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, for non-emergency installations. We confirm the price after evaluating your bathroom's existing plumbing.

Do you install grab bars and accessibility features?

Yes, grab bars, bench seating, and other accessibility features can be included as part of a walk-in conversion, planned in from the start rather than added later.
