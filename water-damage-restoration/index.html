A Different Service Than A Plumbing Repair

Cleanup, Not The Pipe Itself

Water damage restoration is the cleanup and drying of structure and contents after water has already gotten into a home, whether from a burst pipe, storm intrusion, or another source. It is a separate service from repairing the plumbing component that caused the water in the first place. If you also need the actual pipe or fixture fixed, our standard plumbing line at (239) 522-0887 handles that side of it, while this emergency line is dedicated specifically to restoration and drying.

Why This Matters In Fort Myers

Local Storm & Flood Risk

Fort Myers sees real hurricane and heavy rainy-season flood risk, and standing water left untreated for even a day or two in Southwest Florida's heat and humidity creates fast conditions for mold growth. Homes on septic systems can also see drainfield-related water intrusion during extended heavy rain. Acting quickly on extraction and drying is genuinely time-sensitive here, not just a sales point. Older homes with original cast iron plumbing and homes in low-lying or flood-prone parts of Lee County tend to see this most, since aging infrastructure and drainage patterns compound the same underlying water-intrusion risk.

What To Do And Not Do

  • Shut off the water source if it's safe to do so and you know where the shutoff valve is.
  • Move valuable or easily damaged items away from standing water if it's safe to do so.
  • Turn off electricity to any affected area at the breaker if there is any risk of water contacting outlets, switches, or appliances.
  • Avoid using household fans alone to dry a large area. They can spread moisture into drier sections rather than removing it, unlike commercial drying equipment.
  • Photograph the damage before cleanup starts if you plan to file an insurance claim.
  • Keep a written note of when the water damage was first noticed, since insurers often ask for a timeline alongside photos.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is water damage restoration the same as fixing the leak?

No. Restoration is the cleanup and drying of the affected structure and contents. If the plumbing itself, like a burst pipe, still needs repair, that's handled through our standard plumbing line.

Do you respond to water damage calls 24/7?

Yes, this emergency restoration line is answered around the clock, including nights, weekends, and holidays.

How fast should I act after water damage happens?

As soon as it's safe to do so. Florida's heat and humidity accelerate mold risk faster than in drier climates, so extraction and drying within the first day or two matters.

Will my insurance cover water damage restoration?

It depends on the cause. Sudden, accidental water damage is often covered; gradual damage from a long-term undetected leak is frequently treated differently. We recommend confirming directly with your insurer.
