Why Homes Add Filtration

What It Actually Solves

Whole-home water filtration is different from a softener. A softener addresses mineral hardness specifically; filtration targets taste, odor, chlorine levels, and sediment that a softener alone won't touch. Some Fort Myers homes on well water also deal with sediment or mineral content that a whole-home filter handles better than a single under-sink unit. Filtration and softening are often installed together as a combined system when a household wants to address both hardness and taste or sediment concerns at once. Households relying heavily on bottled water for taste reasons often find a whole-home system pays for itself over time compared to ongoing bottled water costs.

Whole-Home vs. Under-Sink

Which Fits Your Situation

Under-Sink Filter

  • Treats one faucet only

  • Simpler installation

Whole-Home System

  • Treats every tap in the house

Before You Call

What To Check First

  • Note whether your main concern is taste, odor, sediment, or a combination, since that affects which system type fits best.
  • Check your water heater and faucets for existing scale, since combined hardness and taste issues sometimes call for a combined softener and filtration setup.
  • If you're on well water, mention that when you call, since well water often needs different filtration stages than municipal supply.
  • If you're already considering a water softener, mention that too, since a combined system is often more cost-effective than two separate installs.
FAQs

Common Questions

Do I need a softener or a filtration system?

A softener addresses mineral hardness specifically; filtration targets taste, odor, chlorine, and sediment. Many homes benefit from both together.

Is a whole-home system hard to maintain?

Most systems just need periodic filter changes on a set schedule, similar in effort to changing an HVAC filter, without daily attention required.

Will whole-home filtration help with well water sediment?

Yes, sediment filtration is one of the more common reasons well-water homes in the area add a whole-home system to protect fixtures and appliances downstream.

How often does the system need maintenance?

Most systems need periodic filter changes, with the exact schedule depending on usage and the specific system installed, which we'll walk you through at installation.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, for non-emergency installations. We confirm the price after evaluating your water and household needs during an on-site visit.

Can filtration remove all contaminants from tap water?

No single system removes everything. We'll be upfront about what a given filtration setup actually addresses for your specific water concerns rather than overselling its scope.
