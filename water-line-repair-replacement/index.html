Low water pressure everywhere, or a wet patch in the yard between the street and your house? That points to the water service line itself, the pipe carrying water from the meter or curb stop into your home, rather than plumbing inside the house. It's a different section of pipe from what a typical leak detection or repiping call addresses.

Why This Matters In Fort Myers

The Local Cause

Older service lines in the Fort Myers area, especially in neighborhoods developed before more recent code updates, sometimes still run galvanized steel or aging copper between the meter and the house. Sandy soil over limestone bedrock can shift slightly over time, which stresses buried service lines at their joints and connection points. A service line issue is different from a leak found inside the home, since it sits underground on the utility side of the property before reaching your fixtures. Because this section of pipe is buried and shared with the utility connection, it's often overlooked until a pressure or billing symptom brings it to attention.

Signs You Need Service

When To Call

Low Pressure Throughout The House

Pressure dropping at every fixture, not just one, often points to the service line rather than an internal pipe.

Wet Or Soggy Yard Patch

An unexplained wet area between the meter and the house can indicate a leak in the buried service line.

Unusually High Water Bill

A rising bill with no change in household usage can point to a service line leak losing water underground.

Meter Still Running With Everything Off

If the meter keeps moving with every fixture in the house shut off, water is escaping somewhere between the meter and the home.

Before You Call

What To Check First

  • Check your water meter with everything in the house turned off. Continued movement confirms an active leak somewhere in the system.
  • Look for an unexplained wet or unusually green patch of yard between the street and your home.
  • Compare recent water bills if available, since a sudden increase with no usage change is a useful clue for a service line issue worth mentioning when you call.
FAQs

Common Questions

How is this different from repiping?

Repiping replaces pipe inside the house. Water line repair addresses the separate underground service line running from the meter or curb stop into your home.

How long does a service line typically last?

Modern PEX or copper service lines can last several decades; older galvanized steel lines are more likely to need attention as they approach or exceed that age.

Do you have to dig up my whole yard?

Not always. Trenchless repair methods can address a service line issue with much less excavation than a full dig, depending on the specific damage.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, for non-emergency evaluations. We confirm the price after inspection.

Could this actually be an internal plumbing leak instead?

It's possible. If the issue turns out to be inside the home rather than the service line itself, our leak detection service covers that situation.
