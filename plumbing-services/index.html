Safety First

What To Do In A Genuine Emergency

Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

Shut off the water at your home's main valve if you know where it is, then call. Don't wait for the flooding to worsen before reaching out.

Smell Gas Anywhere

Leave the area immediately, don't operate switches or open flames, and call your gas utility from outside before calling us for the repair.

Sewage Backup

Avoid running additional water or using affected fixtures until a technician has assessed the line, since that can push the backup further.

No Hot Water Or Total Water Loss

Check that this isn't a wider utility outage first, then call so we can prioritize based on how it's affecting your household.
