Topping off the pool more than evaporation should explain? A pool that's genuinely losing water beyond normal evaporation, roughly a quarter inch to half inch per day in Florida's heat, usually has a leak somewhere in the plumbed equipment lines, the fill valve, or a fitting rather than the pool shell itself.

What We Handle

Plumbing-Side Pool Leaks

Residential pools are common across Fort Myers, and a real share of "pool leak" calls turn out to be plumbing issues rather than a problem with the pool's structure or liner: a cracked equipment line, a failing fill valve, or a leaking fitting where pipe connects to the pad equipment. We handle the plumbing side of pool leak detection and repair, tracing water loss through the equipment pad, fill lines, and connections. A structural leak in the pool shell or liner itself is outside plumbing scope and is better handled by a pool specialist, which we'll tell you honestly if that's what we find. Many Florida homes were built with the pool and house sharing the same water supply and backflow setup, which is part of why a pool-related water loss issue can sometimes trace back to plumbing most homeowners wouldn't think to check first.

Signs Of A Plumbing-Side Leak

What To Look For

Water Loss Beyond Evaporation

Losing noticeably more than a quarter to half inch of water per day points to an actual leak, not just heat and evaporation.

Wet Spots Near Equipment

Consistent moisture around the pump, filter, or heater area often traces back to a plumbing connection.

Fill Valve Running Constantly

An auto-fill valve that runs more than expected can indicate a leak elsewhere in the system triggering it.

Air In The Pump System

Air bubbles at return jets can point to a suction-side leak in the plumbing rather than the pool structure.

Before You Call

What To Check First

  • Do a basic evaporation check: mark the water level, wait 24 hours with the pump off, and compare to a bucket of water left at the same depth nearby.
  • Check for wet spots specifically around the equipment pad, since that's the most common plumbing-side leak location.
  • Note whether water loss happens with the pump on, off, or both, since that helps narrow down pressure-side versus suction-side leaks.
FAQs

Common Questions

How much water loss is normal from evaporation?

Roughly a quarter to half inch per day is typical in Florida's heat. Losing noticeably more than that suggests an actual leak.

Do you fix leaks in the pool shell or liner itself?

No, that's outside plumbing scope and is better handled by a pool specialist. We handle the plumbed equipment lines, fill valves, and fittings.

How do you find a leak in buried pool plumbing?

Similar methods to our standard leak detection service: pressure testing and acoustic tracing to locate the leak without unnecessary digging.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, for non-emergency calls. We confirm the price after tracing the leak.

Can a pool leak affect my regular water bill?

Yes, an auto-fill valve compensating for a leak by adding water regularly can show up as an unexplained increase on your household water bill.
